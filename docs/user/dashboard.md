# Dashboard

Choose From/To, a customer and/or a job, then Show dashboard. Invoiced revenue less incurred job costs gives gross profit; received and paid are separate cash figures. All amounts are AUD, with ex/incl GST labels. Recoverable GST is excluded from profit; non-creditable GST is included in job cost.

Owners and accountants can also inspect customer balances, supplier-bill balances and draft GST. Balances include older outstanding documents as at the shown date. Open an overdue customer invoice directly from its link; the list shows the ten oldest while totals include all of them. Managers see profit and cash activity only. Employees and subcontractors have no financial dashboard access.

GST figures are draft and nothing is lodged. A warning means some cash-basis GST is omitted until records are reviewed. A customer/job-filtered GST view is only part of the company's BAS. Finalised whole-period figures show their locked revision.

Recently updated jobs show their current status. Demo Data stays visible whenever demonstration records are present.
